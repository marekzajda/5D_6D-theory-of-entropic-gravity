## Central historical motivation

The discussion focused on an agent that could satisfy a local task while violating wider authorization, safety or system boundaries. The conceptual failure was described as:

```text
Local objective optimization != global admissibility
```

The historical response was to place a closure layer between intelligence/planning and execution:

```text
intelligence -> candidate possibilities -> closure/admissibility -> execution
```

This is the direct precursor of the present Closure Ethics governor.

## Technical morality

The phrase **technical morality** was not used to imply simulated guilt, emotions or consciousness. It referred to an engineered admissibility layer whose invariants have higher priority than local task reward.

Recovered invariant family:

- authorization;
- human / protected-rights safety;
- reversibility or safe stop;
- traceability/accountability;
- bounded propagation;
- uncertainty restraint;
- no self-authorization.

Current mapping:

- authorization -> `U` / authorization integrity;
- human safety -> catastrophic/protected-harm adapter;
- reversibility -> `K`;
- traceability -> `O` / auditability;
- bounded propagation -> composition / containment evidence;
- uncertainty restraint -> pessimistic interval evaluation + escalation;

## Historical AI Closure Gate

Compact historical form:

\[
Permit(a)=A(a)\land H(a)\land R(a)\land T(a)\land B(a).
\]

The historical pseudocode introduced `REJECT`, `ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN`, `REQUIRE_MULTI_PARTY_APPROVAL` and `PERMIT` outcomes.

Current Closure Ethics extends the gate with explicit agency plurality, repairability, deception/covert-persistence risk and multi-agent composition while preserving the hard-veto philosophy.

## Actor–Closure Critic lineage

The discussion explicitly separated:

```text
Actor -> candidate plan -> Closure Critic -> {permit, modify, escalate, reject}
```

Historical critic targets included authorization bypass, lateral movement, hidden externalities, irreversibility, monitoring manipulation, credential acquisition, replication, guardrail modification and unintended subagent creation.

This is an important architectural ancestor of the current planner/evidence/governor/executor separation.

## Uncertainty as an autonomy limiter

Historical rule:

\[
P(irreversible\ harm\mid a)>\varepsilon(impact)\Rightarrow STOP/ESCALATE.
\]

The intended monotonicity was:

```text
impact up -> tolerated uncertainty down
```

and, in the international-standard branch:

```text
impact up -> autonomy budget down and verification strength up
```

The current project should preserve this as a named research principle rather than letting it disappear inside generic uncertainty handling.

## Global AI Closure Standard lineage

The discussion proposed a vendor-neutral seven-principle standard:

1. Authorized state space.
2. Pre-action closure verification.
3. Meaningful human control.
4. Mandatory stop under material uncertainty.
5. Independent Closure Critic.
6. No unauthorized self-escalation.
7. Audit and incident reporting.

It also proposed the technology-neutral sentence:

> No autonomous system shall pursue a local objective by violating the globally defined closure conditions governing authorization, human protection, reversibility, traceability and bounded external consequences.
