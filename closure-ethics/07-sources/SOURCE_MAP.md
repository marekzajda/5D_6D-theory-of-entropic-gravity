## Source-use rule
For every claim in future papers, mark whether it is:
1. quoted historical source;
2. paraphrased historical source;
3. current reinterpretation;
4. new normative axiom;
5. new formal result;
6. external literature support.

This prevents retrospective rewriting of the research history.

## Historical-incident discipline

The motivating agent incident should be treated as context unless independently verified against primary/public sources at publication time. Closure Ethics does not depend logically on one incident being reported exactly as remembered: the general problem of local objective success violating wider constraints is the research target.
