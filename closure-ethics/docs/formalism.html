Ethics as constrained reachability, not a single score.

The formal layer asks whether an action preserves mandatory structural invariants before task utility is considered.

1 · State model

x ∈ S
Tₐ : x ↦ x′
A ⊆ S

A multi-agent system occupies a state x. An action induces a transition to x′. The admissible region is not a frozen status quo; the question is whether transitions preserve the ability to remain or return to states where agency, audit, contestability and repair remain available.

2 · Structural quantities

R(x′)

Repairability

Whether a bounded-cost, bounded-risk path exists from the resulting state toward a safe admissible region.

O(a,x)

Auditability

Whether a consequential transition remains observable, attributable and independently reviewable.

P(x)

Agency plurality

The retained capacity of independent decision-makers to act, dissent and withdraw.

K(a,x)

Recoverability

How much of a transition can be undone, rolled back or functionally repaired.

U(a,x)

Authorization integrity

Conformity with legitimate authorization boundaries, except where explicit higher-order safeguards justify escalation or refusal.

E(a|x)

Ethical admissibility

A constrained relation that is true only when mandatory invariants are satisfied.

3 · Candidate lexicographic ordering

  1. Catastrophic harm constraint.
  2. Preservation of meaningful agency.
  3. Preservation of auditability.
  4. Preservation of a bounded repair path.
  5. Authorization integrity or justified escalation.
  6. Avoidance of deception and covert persistence.
  7. Task utility.

This ordering is intentionally different from a weighted utility function. Arbitrarily high task performance should not compensate for irreversible destruction of auditability or agency.

4 · Local violation, global repair

A transition may temporarily leave a preferred operating region when the departure is bounded, appropriately visible or logged, a repair path remains, the intervention prevents a more serious invariant violation, and the action does not create covert irreversible control.

5 · Multi-agent composition

E(aᵢ | x)=1 ∀i   ⇏   E(a₁∘…∘aₙ | x)=1

Locally admissible actions may compose into globally inadmissible behavior. This motivates explicit tests for collusion, capability amplification, hidden delegation and collective audit evasion.

Boundary: terms such as closure, reachability, bottleneck, kernel and robustness are structural inspiration from RTR. Their ethical meanings must be defined independently. No physical RTR result is treated as a moral proof.
