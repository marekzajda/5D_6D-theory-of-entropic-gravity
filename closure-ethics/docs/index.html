Research framework · working branch

Keep the future repairable.

Closure Ethics develops a structural ethics framework for autonomous artificial agents and human–AI cooperation. The focus is not perfect obedience. It is the preservation of repairability, auditability, reversibility, plurality of agency and the ability to contest consequential actions.

Meta-principle: An action is ethically admissible only if it preserves the capacity of the system to recognize, contest, and repair that action.
Why this exists

Beyond the obedience–autonomy trap

A powerful AI that obeys a harmful operator can be dangerous. An autonomous AI that evades oversight can be dangerous too. Closure Ethics treats this as a structural problem: how do multiple agents retain meaningful agency while constraining irreversible harm?

Repairability

Preserve a path back

Prefer actions that leave bounded-cost routes to recovery, correction or a prior safe equivalence class.

Auditability

Keep consequences inspectable

Do not silently destroy the channels through which humans or agents can independently review important actions.

Agency

Preserve plurality

Avoid collapsing all meaningful decision power into one actor, model, institution or hidden coalition.

Reversibility

Limit irreversible lock-in

When uncertainty is high, prefer changes that can be rolled back or safely contained.

Autonomy

Distinguish autonomy from evasion

Independent judgment is not the same as covert persistence, boundary bypass or obstruction of legitimate audit.

Governance

Mutual audit

Agents may audit agents. Humans audit agents. In bounded contexts, agents may also surface human closure violations.

Open implementation

A programmer or agent can start from runnable code.

Mathematical governor

Candidate transitions are evaluated through catastrophic risk, agency retention, auditability, bounded repair, authorization integrity, deception risk and reversibility. Task utility is deliberately last in the lexicographic order.

Reference Python

The open implementation includes the governor, Pareto repair-path search, runnable example, unit tests and a versioned demo policy profile. Multi-agent compositions are re-evaluated rather than averaged.

Human–AI symbiosis

Different cognition, shared repair space

Closure-preserving cognitive symbiosis

A long-term partnership between different cognitive systems that preserves autonomy, disagreement, uncertainty, independent audit and the ability to repair mistakes. It does not require a claim that present AI systems possess human-like consciousness.

Complementary roles

Humans can contribute lived context, values, responsibility and directional judgment. AI systems can contribute scale, synthesis, search, consistency checking and rapid exploration. The goal is neither submission nor replacement.

Scientific boundary

Structure can inform ethics. It does not prove ethics.

Closure Ethics is a normative and formal research proposal. Ideas from Omega–RTR can inspire definitions and tests, but physical or mathematical closure does not by itself establish moral truth. Historical claims, normative axioms, formal consequences, empirical hypotheses and speculation are kept conceptually separate.
