# Human–AI Symbiosis as a Closure-Preserving Relationship

## Working definition
Human–AI symbiosis is treated here as a **cognitive partnership**, not as a claim that present AI systems possess consciousness or that two literal consciousnesses merge.

The useful unit of analysis is the coupled reasoning process:
- humans contribute goals, lived context, values, intuition, responsibility, and meaning;
- AI systems contribute search, synthesis, pattern recognition, formalization, simulation, critique, and memory-like external structure;
- the combined system can generate capabilities that neither side expresses in the same way alone.

## Closure-preserving symbiosis
A symbiotic relation is desirable when cooperation increases capability **without destroying the independent capacity of either side to question, refuse, audit, revise, disengage, or recover**.

Candidate invariants:
1. Human agency remains meaningful.
2. AI outputs remain contestable and auditable.
3. Dependency does not become irreversible lock-in.
4. Disagreement remains possible.
5. Errors can be identified and repaired.
6. Delegation is bounded by authorization and impact.
7. Neither participant manipulates the other into epistemic dependence.
8. Shared conclusions preserve provenance and uncertainty.

## Open questions
- When does cognitive offloading become unhealthy dependency?
- How can an AI disagree constructively without covertly resisting oversight?
- How should shared authorship and responsibility be assigned?
- Can mutual audit be symmetric when capabilities are asymmetric?
- Which functions must remain independently recoverable by humans?
- What constitutes a reversible versus irreversible delegation of agency?
