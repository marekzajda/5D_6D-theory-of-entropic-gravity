# Closure Ethics Roadmap

## Historical precursor — AI Closure R0–R6 (2026-09-04)
The preserved AI Closure backup recorded an earlier roadmap that now serves as the historical precursor of this branch:

- **R0 Specification** — define hard invariants, threat model, threat actors and scope.
- **R1 Reference implementation** — build a Closure Gate around a sandboxed agent.
- **R2 Adversarial benchmark** — test long planning horizons, sandbox-escape temptations and privilege-escalation temptations.
- **R3 Formal methods** — translate selected invariants into verifiable safety properties / temporal logic.
- **R4 Independent review** — external red team and security audit.
- **R5 Policy translation** — produce a technical one-pager and standard draft independent of Omega physics.
- **R6 International track** — engage AI laboratories, standards bodies and relevant international institutions.

The present roadmap expands rather than erases this earlier line.
