# Project Scope

## Scope
Closure Ethics is a research branch exploring structural conditions for safe, plural, auditable, and repairable interaction among autonomous agents and humans.

## Non-goals
- It does not claim that a physical theory proves moral truth.
- It does not assume present AI systems are conscious.
- It does not equate obedience with safety.
- It does not attempt to eliminate disagreement or autonomy.
- It does not treat stability as preservation of the status quo.

## Governance invariant
Changes should preserve:
1. traceability;
2. rollback;
3. explicit distinction between source material and new interpretation;
4. explicit distinction between normative axioms and formal consequences;
5. independent auditability.

## Branch policy
The `closure-ethics` branch is exploratory. Merge to `main` only after review of terminology, claims, citations, and formal consistency.
